But descriptions of the human being and human activity are studied not only by psychology. Here are a few more ideas. First, we should study and advance not sciences but problems. For the most interesting problems nobody solves — we will be first. Second, we should cut off problems from which the human can be taken "out of brackets" — and give those problems to natural science, which already handles them well. And third, somehow group and order the problems, for example place them on a two-dimensional plane. I prefer two axes: (think — do) left to right and (many of us — I alone) bottom to top. Compare with Ken Wilber's plane (further on).

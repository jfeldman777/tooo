Psychology has a certain number of models (from different producers) and a certain number of observed patterns (from different observers).

Unfortunately, the owners of models collect the data themselves (and
there is little of it), and ignore other people's data.

Conversely, the owners of observations cling to their special language the way an artist clings to a special style and recognizability.

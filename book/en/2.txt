What is a field of attention?
Before cooking my favorite dish, I lay out the ingredients and tools on the kitchen table in the right order — so I forget nothing and do not improvise — and only then, looking at the prepared and ordered ingredients and tools, do I begin to cook.
With this approach the chance that I skip some step tends toward zero.
In this case the kitchen table is my field of attention. But in life there are other, less comfortable situations
